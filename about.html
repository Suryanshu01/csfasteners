About C S Fasteners

An MSME-registered screw manufacturer in Chandigarh, India, supplying self tapping, machine, wood, chipboard and custom fasteners to OEMs, assemblers, importers and distributors worldwide.

Request a Quote → WhatsApp Us

Who we are

C S Fasteners manufactures industrial screws from our facility in Industrial Area Phase-1, Chandigarh. We are registered as an MSME manufacturer under the Government of India's Udyam scheme, and we supply the domestic Indian market and quote export orders on FOB and CIF terms.

Our work divides into two halves. The first is standard production: self tapping, machine, wood and chipboard screws manufactured to DIN and ISO dimensional standards, in the sizes and finishes buyers specify most often. The second is made-to-order work: parts produced to a customer's drawing or physical sample where a catalogue item will not do, including confidential white-label supply under the customer's own brand.

Finishes

We supply seven finish families, chosen by the service environment rather than by habit. Zinc and yellow zinc for general and indoor use; black oxide and phosphate where appearance matters; nickel and chrome for visible and conductive parts; and the zinc-flake systems Dacromet, Geo-met and Ruspert for exterior, coastal and high-strength applications where salt-spray performance and freedom from hydrogen embrittlement both matter.

If you tell us the environment rather than the finish, we will recommend one. The finishes guide sets out the comparison.

How we work with buyers

  • Flexible minimum order quantities. We would rather run a small first order and earn repeat business than turn away a development project.
  • Made to drawing or sample. Send a dimensioned drawing or a physical part. Where there is no drawing, two or three samples let us separate design intent from manufacturing variation.
  • Written confirmation before tooling. We confirm standard, property class, thread tolerance and finish back to you before anything is cut, so there is no ambiguity about what arrives.
  • Confidential white-label supply. Your cartons, your labelling, your part numbers. Nothing identifies us as the manufacturer and we do not disclose customer relationships.
  • Export-ready. FOB and CIF terms with full documentation. Our importing guide covers MOQ, lead times and Incoterms for first-time importers.

Standards

We manufacture to the common DIN and ISO dimensional standards so parts integrate into an existing bill of materials without re-qualification, and hold thread tolerance to 6g as standard, which leaves correct clearance for a plated or coated finish without binding. Property classes follow ISO 898-1. The full cross reference is in our DIN and ISO standards guide.
