FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of screws does C S Fasteners offer?
C S Fasteners supplies self-tapping screws (2.9–6.3mm), machine screws (M2–M16), wood screws (3–8mm), chipboard screws (3–6mm), and fully custom screws made to drawing or sample.
Does C S Fasteners offer white-label or OEM manufacturing?
Yes, C S Fasteners offers white-label manufacturing where screws are produced under the buyer's brand name with custom packaging. Full confidentiality is maintained.
What finishes are available for screws?
C S Fasteners offers Zinc Plated, Yellow Zinc, Black Oxide, Dacromet, Geo-met, Ruspert, Nickel, Chrome, and Phosphate finishes.
Does C S Fasteners export screws internationally?
Yes, C S Fasteners is an export-ready MSME manufacturer based in Chandigarh, India. We offer FOB and CIF shipping terms with full export documentation.
What packaging options does C S Fasteners offer?
C S Fasteners offers multiple packaging options: cardboard boxes, plastic boxes, plastic bags, and standard industrial bags. Custom packaging with buyer branding is available for white-label orders.
