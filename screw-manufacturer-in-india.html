We manufacture, we do not stock and resell

This distinction matters more than it sounds, because a large share of the businesses that appear under "screw manufacturer in India" are stockists and trading houses. They hold inventory across a very wide catalogue of alloys and fastener types and ship what is on the shelf. That model is genuinely useful when you need one box of an exotic alloy fastener tomorrow.

It is the wrong model when you need a specific part made to a specific standard, repeatably, in volume, with the same thread form and the same head geometry on the ten thousandth piece as on the first.

We run production at our own facility at 136-140/17, Industrial Area Phase-1, Chandigarh. That means we control the thread rolling, the head forming, the heat treatment and the finish, rather than selecting the nearest match from stock. It also means we can make a part that does not exist in any catalogue, from your drawing or a physical sample.

Our range is deliberately narrower than a stockist's and considerably deeper within it. We do not claim to supply Inconel, Hastelloy or Monel screws. We make carbon steel, case-hardened, alloy steel, stainless and brass screws extremely well.

Mechanical properties by property class

Property class marks the strength of a steel fastener under ISO 898-1. The first number is one hundredth of the nominal tensile strength in megapascals; the second is ten times the ratio of yield to tensile strength.

ClassTensile strength (MPa)Yield strength (MPa, approx)Material familyTypical application

10.91000900Alloy steel, quenched and temperedAutomotive, structural, high load
12.912001080Alloy steel, quenched and temperedSocket cap screws, highest standard grade

Stainless fasteners are graded separately under ISO 3506, written as A2-70 or A4-80, where the letter gives the steel group and the number is one tenth of the tensile strength in MPa. Full explanation in our DIN and ISO standards reference.

A note on high-strength fasteners: above class 10.9, electroplating introduces atomic hydrogen into the steel and can cause delayed brittle failure. For those classes we recommend a zinc-flake finish such as Dacromet or Geo-met, which is cured thermally rather than deposited electrolytically, so the embrittlement mechanism never begins.

Heads, drives and finishes

We produce the great majority of standard head profiles: countersunk, raised countersunk, pan, bugle, truss, cheese, fillister, button, wafer, pancake, hex, hex flange, hex washer and socket cap, plus non-standard geometry to drawing. The complete list with selection guidance is in the head types chart.

Drive recesses cover Philips, Pozidriv, Torx, square, internal hex and slotted. For powered and automated assembly we usually recommend Torx, whose near-vertical flanks generate almost no cam-out, which cuts bit replacement cost and reject rates. Comparison and a bit size chart are in the drive types guide.

Quality and consistency

We manufacture to DIN and ISO dimensional standards so parts integrate into an existing bill of materials without re-qualification. For first orders we produce first-off samples for your dimensional and functional approval before the production run, and we confirm standard, property class, thread tolerance and finish back to you in writing before any tooling is cut.

FAQ

Screw Manufacturing FAQ

What screw sizes do you manufacture in India?
Machine screws M2 to M16 (6 to 200 mm), self tapping 2.9 to 6.3 mm (9 to 100 mm), wood screws 3 to 8 mm (12 to 150 mm) and chipboard screws 3 to 6 mm (15 to 200 mm). Sizes outside these ranges are produced to drawing on our custom line.
Are you a screw manufacturer or a stockist?
We manufacture. Production runs on our own line in Industrial Area Phase-1, Chandigarh, so we control thread form, head geometry, heat treatment and finish rather than selecting from stock, and we can make non-standard parts to a drawing or sample.
Which standards do you manufacture screws to?
The common DIN and ISO dimensional standards including DIN 84 / ISO 1207, DIN 85 / ISO 1580, DIN 963 / ISO 2009, DIN 965 / ISO 7046, DIN 7985 / ISO 7045, DIN 912 / ISO 4762 and the DIN 7981 / ISO 7049 tapping series. Property classes follow ISO 898-1, stainless follows ISO 3506, thread tolerance is 6g as standard.
What is the minimum order quantity?
Flexible and project dependent. We would rather run a small first order and earn repeat business than turn away a development project.
Do you export screws from India?
Yes, on FOB and CIF terms with full documentation, and with confidential white-label packaging under your own brand where required.
