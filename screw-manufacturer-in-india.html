Materials, head styles and finishes

Our range includes mild and carbon steel screws, with case-hardened options for self-tapping applications. Choose from head styles such as pan, countersunk and hex flange, and drive types such as Phillips, Pozidriv, Torx, slotted and internal hex.

Options include zinc, yellow zinc, black oxide and black phosphate, nickel, chrome, Dacromet, Geo-met and Ruspert. Use the finishes guide to compare coatings, and share your material grade and service environment when you request a quote.

Samples and production orders

For a first production run, we confirm the drawing, finish and acceptance criteria before tooling. You can check the sample for fit and function in your assembly before approving the bulk run.

Applications by industry

  • Furniture and woodworking: flat-pack, carcass assembly, joinery and cabinet hardware.
  • Construction and roofing: cladding, purlin fixing, drywall and structural timber.
  • Automotive applications include trim, panels and brackets.
  • Electronics and appliances: small-diameter precision fixing, chassis and moulded housings.
  • HVAC and sheet metal: ducting, enclosures, trunking and panel work.

Bulk orders and white-label packaging

We supply customers across India from Chandigarh. OEMs and distributors can request white-label screw manufacturing with branded cartons, labels and part numbers. For overseas orders, see our guide to importing screws from India for shipping terms and documentation.

What to include in a quote request

  • Part specification. Send the DIN or ISO number or a dimensioned drawing with its revision. Include the diameter, length, thread pitch, head and drive.
  • Material and finish. Give the material grade or property class, coating requirements and any inspection reports your order requires.
  • Quantity and delivery. List the quantity per size, sample requirements, delivery destination and requested delivery date.
  • Packaging. Include units per pack and any private-label artwork or part numbers.

Use the quote form or email drawings to sales@csfasteners.com. If you are still choosing a screw, tell us what materials you are joining and how you will install it.

FAQ

Screw Manufacturing FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity depends on the part, tooling and finish. Send the specification and quantity so we can confirm the practical batch size and lead time.
Can you match an existing screw from a sample?
Yes. Send a physical sample and describe how the screw is used. Two or three samples help us check which dimensions vary before we confirm the specification.
Can you quote export orders from India?
Yes. We quote on FOB and CIF terms. Send your specification, quantity and destination port so we can confirm the documentation and packaging for your order.
